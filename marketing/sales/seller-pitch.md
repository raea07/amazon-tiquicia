# Seller pitch

**What it is.** Everything needed to ask a shop to join the pilot: a five-minute visit, a one-minute message, a one-page leave-behind, a ten-slide deck, objections and answers, the price sheet from the product's real fees, and the seller's first week. Spanish (usted) and English side by side.
**Conclusion 1.** The pitch sells time and a delivery someone answers for, not a lower price. The fees are shown in full, with a worked sale, before the shop is asked for anything.
**Conclusion 2.** Every fee here is the product's schedule `2026-11-assumptions`, labelled "tarifas del piloto". They can change before the pilot; the sheet says so.
**Conclusion 3.** The ask is small and honest: join a closed pilot, with five shops, before any real sale has happened.
**The owner must decide** the two questions at the end (the minimum to join, who visits). The joining offer is the pricing proposal's decision 4; the payout day is README decision 3.

**Gates** ([README](../README.md#the-gates)): G1 and G2 before any visit under the name; interviews without printed material may start earlier. G8 before promising the warehouse. G4 before saying the money is collected for real. G5 and the accountant before saying the IVA can be credited. G12 for the lines on returns and on return costs.

### 6.3 Collection / Cobro

On the price of your products in the order (never on the delivery charge). The fixed part is charged once per order and shared between the shops in it. The schedule's figures are below; print the range beside them until a gateway is contracted (`pricing-and-fees.md`).

| Medio de pago / Payment method    | Hoy en el sistema / In the schedule |                                           Rango a imprimir / Range to print |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
| SINPE Móvil                       |                         1,5% / 1.5% |      1,5% a 2%, a veces + cargo fijo / 1.5% to 2%, sometimes + a fixed part |
| Tarjeta / Card (open launch)      |           3,9% + ₡180 / 3.9% + ₡180 |                    3,9% a 4,25% + cargo fijo / 3.9% to 4.25% + a fixed part |
| Contra entrega / Cash on delivery |                           2% + ₡300 | 2% + ₡300 (supuesto, sin precio de pasarela / assumption, no gateway price) |

Plus **13% IVA on every fee above** (commission, warehouse, collection).

Range: the two gateway prices in the fact sheet §5 and the pricing proposal §2.6 (ONVO as a third party reports it: SINPE Móvil 1.5%, card 3.9% + US$0.35; Tilopay's page: SINPE Móvil 2% + US$0.35, card 4.25% + US$0.35), low-to-medium confidence. The card's ₡180 stands for the gateway's US$0.35 at about ₡515 per dollar; at ₡450 it is about ₡158 (market research §6.4). The central bank caps local card acquiring at 1.95% (pricing proposal §2.2, for the lawyer to confirm), so the card line may fall. The card fee is the pricing proposal's decision 2; cards are not in the pilot.

## Decisions for the owner

The joining offer (price lock to a fixed date and 20 products set up) is asked once, as the pricing proposal's decision 4; the leave-behind keeps its bracket until then.

1. **What must a shop have to join the pilot?**
   - A. Products with barcodes, electronic invoicing (the Régimen Simplificado only once the accountant answers compliance §10 q6), and an IBAN account in the shop's name. **(recommended)** Each is needed for a legal, scannable sale today, and the go-to-market's seller tests ask the same.
   - B. The same, but let makers without barcodes join with shop-packed orders arranged one by one, and accept an account in the owner's own name after staff check it.
2. **Who does the visits?**
   - A. The owner for the first 15, then the sales agent with this script. **(recommended)** The roadmap wants the founder to hear the first objections.
   - B. A sales agent from the start.

# Seller pitch

**What it is.** Everything needed to ask a shop to join the pilot: a five-minute visit, a one-minute message, a one-page leave-behind, a ten-slide deck, objections and answers, the price sheet from the product's real fees, and the seller's first week. Spanish (usted) and English side by side.
**Conclusion 1.** The pitch sells time and a delivery someone answers for, not a lower price. The fees are shown in full, with a worked sale, before the shop is asked for anything.
**Conclusion 2.** Every fee here is the product's schedule `2026-11-assumptions`, labelled "tarifas del piloto". They can change before the pilot; the sheet says so.
**Conclusion 3.** The ask is small and honest: join a closed pilot, with five shops, before any real sale has happened.
**The owner must decide** the three questions at the end (the offer, the minimum to join, who visits).

**Gates** ([README](../README.md#the-gates)): G1 and G2 before any visit under the name. G8 before promising the warehouse. G4 before saying the money is collected for real. G12 for the lines on returns.

**Format.** Carta (21.6 × 27.9 cm), portrait, printed both sides. Side 1 Spanish, side 2 English. Bleed 3 mm on every edge; keep text and the QR code at least 5 mm inside the trim (assumption: ask the print shop for its own spec). Paper: 150 g/m² matte or heavier, so it survives a counter (assumption; confirm with the print quote).

## 6. The price sheet, as the seller sees it

Source: schedule `2026-11-assumptions`, active, in `supabase/migrations/20261121080800_finance.sql` and `20261121080450_catalog_reference.sql`, mirrored in `packages/core/src/fees.ts`. **Every amount is a working assumption to validate with pilot sellers** (`pricing-and-fees.md`). Re-check the console before printing.

### 6.1 Commission when you sell / Comisión cuando vende

Percentage of the price the buyer pays (IVA included). At least ₡150 per unit, never more than the price.

| Categoría | Category | Comisión |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Electrónica; Computación; Celulares y accesorios; Abarrotes y café | Electronics; Computers; Phones & accessories; Groceries & coffee | 8% |
| Electrodomésticos; Hecho en Costa Rica | Appliances; Made in Costa Rica | 10% |
| Hogar y cocina; Ferretería y herramientas; Deportes y aire libre; Juguetes y juegos; Mascotas; Libros y oficina | Home & kitchen; Hardware & tools; Sports & outdoors; Toys & games; Pets; Books & office | 12% |
| Belleza y cuidado personal | Beauty & personal care | 13% |
| Moda | Fashion | 15% |
| Cualquier otra | Any other | 12% |

### 6.2 Warehouse, per unit sold / Bodega, por unidad vendida

Only when the product is stored in Yuntera's warehouse. Covers storage, picking, packing and the packaging.

| Tamaño / Size | Hasta / Up to | Lado más largo / Longest side | Cargo |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sobre / Envelope | 500 g | 30 cm | ₡900 |
| Pequeño / Small | 2 kg | 45 cm | ₡1 400 |
| Mediano / Medium | 10 kg | 60 cm | ₡2 400 |
| Grande / Large | 30 kg | 120 cm | ₡4 900 |

Larger than _Grande_: not accepted in the warehouse in the pilot.

### 6.3 Collection / Cobro

On the price of your products in the order (never on the delivery charge). The fixed part is charged once per order and shared between the shops in it.

| Medio de pago / Payment method | Cargo |
| --- | ---: |
| SINPE Móvil | 1,5% / 1.5% |
| Tarjeta / Card | 3,9% + ₡180 / 3.9% + ₡180 |
| Contra entrega / Cash on delivery | 2% + ₡300 |

Plus **13% IVA on every fee above** (commission, warehouse, collection).

The card's ₡180 stands for the gateway's US$0.35 at about ₡515 per dollar; at ₡450 it is about ₡158 (market research §6.4). The pricing proposal recommends keeping ₡180 until a gateway contract fixes the rate and saying so openly (its decision 2). If the owner agrees, the sheet adds: "El cargo fijo de tarjeta cubre los cambios del tipo de cambio hasta que firmemos el contrato con la pasarela. / The card's fixed fee covers exchange-rate moves until we sign the gateway contract."

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Is there a joining offer on the leave-behind?**
   - A. The 6-month price lock and free set-up of the first 20 products, with a join-by date. **(recommended)** It is the pricing proposal's recommendation and costs staff time, not margin.
   - B. No offer.
2. **What must a shop have to join the pilot?**
   - A. Products with barcodes, electronic invoicing or the simplified regime, and an IBAN account in the shop's name. **(recommended)** Each is needed for a legal, scannable sale today.
   - B. The same, but let makers without barcodes join with shop-packed orders only, arranged one by one.
3. **Who does the visits?**
   - A. The owner for the first 15, then the sales agent with this script. **(recommended)** The roadmap wants the founder to hear the first objections.
   - B. A sales agent from the start.

# Notice: drivers

**What it is.** A notice to recruit the first one or two drivers for the pilot routes, posted where drivers and messengers gather or handed out, with the copy and its blanks.
**Conclusion 1.** It may not be used until the lawyer has said in writing how drivers are engaged and an INS work-risk policy exists (gate G11). The pay, the contract type and the vehicle line are blanks until then.
**Conclusion 2.** What it can promise today is how the work is done: an ordered route, delivery proved by the customer's code, cash counted with the driver.
**Conclusion 3.** Code RUTA-1 counts replies; the notice asks for no personal data in the first message.
**The owner must decide** nothing here. How drivers are engaged, whose vehicle, and the first hire are the team structure's decisions 1 and 2.

**Gates:** G1, G2, G9, G11, G12. The founder drives in the pilot (team structure §5), and drivers are added only when a zone's volume fills a route (`business-model.md`), so this notice may not be needed until after the pilot.

## What the picture shows

A motorbike or small car parked at a kerb in an ordinary GAM street, a parcel in a top box or on the seat, a phone in a holder showing a route list (an invented screen with no real names or addresses). No faces needed. No other company's logo or uniform.

Facts: route ordering, pin, señas, call button, code check, cash total and the counted close-out (`STATUS.md`, Routes and warehouse; `logistics-playbook.md`, Delivery and Daily cash reconciliation). Location during active routes is recorded only with the driver's consent (`privacy.md`, "Repartidores"); say so at the interview, not on the notice.

**Pay, for the owner, not for the notice.** If the lawyer says drivers are employees, the 2026 legal minimum for "Conductor de vehículo liviano" is ₡13,523.69 a day, rising 1.70% on 1 January 2027 (MTSS list, Decreto 45303-MTSS; MTSS communiqué CP-022-2026; market research §6.1). Whether a motorbike courier is that occupation or "Mensajero" (₡373,092.30 a month) is **for the lawyer or accountant to confirm**. Never print a pay figure below the legal minimum for the occupation the lawyer names.

**Wording rules.** No "trabaje cuando quiera", "sea su propio jefe" or "gane hasta …": they presume an engagement basis or an income nobody has measured (compliance §7; brand book open item 4 removed a tagline for the same reason). No age, sex or nationality requirement; whether any requirement beyond the licence is lawful is for the lawyer to confirm.

## How the result is measured

| Measure | Where |
| --- | --- |

| Interviews held, people engaged | Owner's list (names kept only for candidates who consented, deleted if not engaged; for the lawyer to set the period) |
| Done when | Routes covered for 2 weeks with a trained backup (roadmap phase 1 risk) |

## What this notice waits for

These are asked once, in the team structure (`docs/00-strategy/team-structure.md`). The notice fills its blanks from the answers.

| Question | Where | What it changes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How drivers are engaged (employee with INS cover, contractor, or a courier company), and whose vehicle | Team structure, decision 1 | The engagement, pay and vehicle lines. If the driver brings a motorbike, the ₡45,000 driver-day is worked out again, the occupation may be "Mensajero", and vehicle ownership bears on employment status (compliance §7), **for the lawyer to confirm** |
| The first hire after the backup person | Team structure, decision 2 | When the notice is posted. In the pilot the founder drives (team structure §5); post it when that decision picks a driver and a zone fills a route most days |

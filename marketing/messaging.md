# Message house

**What it is.** The words every Yuntera piece is built from: one sentence for the company, then for each audience the problem, the promise, three proofs, the objection and the honest answer. Spanish (usted) and English side by side.
**Conclusion 1.** Yuntera sells reliability, not price: usually the next delivery day in named cantons, with the exact date shown before paying; SINPE Móvil and cash; and someone local who answers.
**Conclusion 2.** Every proof below is a feature built in the product today; what depends on a gate is marked (gates in [README](README.md#the-gates)).
**Conclusion 3.** The weakest point for every audience is that nothing has run for real yet. Say so plainly instead of hiding it.
**The owner must decide** the two questions at the end: the company sentence and the tagline to use.

## The company in one sentence

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |
| Yuntera es una tienda en línea para Costa Rica con bodega y repartidores propios: las tiendas del país venden ahí, y nosotros guardamos, cobramos y entregamos. | Yuntera is an online marketplace for Costa Rica with its own warehouse and drivers: local shops sell on it, and we store, collect payment and deliver. |

Short version, for a header or a bio (160 characters at most):

| Español | English |
| --- | --- |
| Tiendas de Costa Rica, a su puerta. Pague con SINPE Móvil o contra entrega. | Costa Rican shops, at your door. Pay by SINPE Móvil or cash on delivery. |

Tagline (from `packages/brand`, brand book §1): **Lo que ocupa, a su puerta.** / _What you need, at your door._ The brand book asks a native copy editor to read the three secondary taglines before public use (open item 4).

La fecha exacta la ve antes de pagar. Pague con SINPE Móvil o contra entrega. Si algo sale mal, nosotros respondemos y lo resolvemos. | For items in our warehouse, order Monday to Saturday before 2 p.m. and it usually arrives the next delivery day, in [cantons]. You see the exact date before you pay. Pay by SINPE Móvil or cash on delivery. If something goes wrong, we take care of it. |
| **Proof 1** | Antes de pagar ve la fecha de entrega y el total con IVA y envío. | Before you pay you see the delivery date and the total with IVA and delivery. |
| **Proof 2** | El repartidor le pide un código de entrega de cuatro dígitos que solo usted ve en su pedido. | The driver asks for a four-digit delivery code that only you can see on your order. |

| **The objection** | "Amazon me lo manda gratis." | "Amazon ships it to me for free." |
| **The honest answer** | En compras pequeñas de productos importados, otras tiendas pueden salir más baratas. Nosotros traemos lo que está en el país, por lo general se lo dejamos el siguiente día de entrega, y le respondemos aquí si algo falla. Where each proof lives: the date and total on the product page and checkout (`STATUS.md`, Shop; compliance §1); the code (`STATUS.md`, "The delivery code"); returns and refunds (help page; `customer-service.md`). The promise needs G4 and G7, and its conditions come from `promise.ts`: a seller-shipped item adds handling days, a Sunday order arrives on Tuesday, and a full day or a zone closure moves the date (go-to-market §3 uses the same sentence). The return exceptions are the help page's. Amazon Bazaar's ₡10,000 free-shipping minimum and two-week delivery are from El Financiero, Nov 2025 (market research §3.2); do not name Amazon in any piece.

## Makers who have never sold online

A craft workshop, a coffee or food producer, a small manufacturer who sells at fairs and to people they know.

| | Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The problem** | "No sé hacer una tienda en línea." "No tengo quién entregue." "Me da miedo que no me paguen." | "I don't know how to build an online shop." "I have nobody to deliver." "I'm afraid I won't get paid." |
| **The promise** | Usted hace el producto. Nosotros le ayudamos a publicarlo, lo guardamos, lo entregamos y le pagamos por transferencia. | You make the product. We help you list it, store it, deliver it and pay you by transfer. |
| **Proof 1** | No paga mensualidad: solo paga una comisión cuando vende. En el piloto, artesanía (_Hecho en Costa Rica_) paga 10%, y café y alimentos empacados (_Abarrotes y café_) 8%. | There is no monthly fee: you pay a commission only when you sell. In the pilot, crafts (_Made in Costa Rica_) pay 10%, and coffee and packaged food (_Groceries & coffee_) 8%. |
| **Proof 2** | Su tienda tiene su propia página, con su descripción y su logotipo. | Your shop has its own page, with your description and your logo. |
| **Proof 3** | El comprador ve el nombre de su tienda, y usted ve solo el nombre y el distrito del comprador: nadie reparte datos personales. | The buyer sees your shop's name, and you see only the buyer's first name and district: nobody hands out personal data. |
| **The objection** | "No tengo código de barras ni factura electrónica." | "I have no barcode and no electronic invoice." |
| **The honest answer** | En el piloto, para guardar su producto en la bodega, cada unidad necesita un código de barras. La factura de su venta la emite usted desde su sistema; si está en el Régimen Simplificado, lo revisamos con nuestro contador antes de que publique. Si todavía no tiene eso, le explicamos qué falta y le avisamos cuando podamos etiquetar nosotros. | In the pilot, to store your product in our warehouse, each unit needs a barcode. You issue the invoice for your sale from your own system; if you are in the simplified tax regime, we check it with our accountant before you list. If you don't have these yet, we explain what is missing and tell you when we can label for you. |

Proof sources: no monthly fee, `hecho-en-cr` at 10% (its only sub-category is Artesanía) and `abarrotes` at 8% (with the sub-category Café) (`pricing-and-fees.md`; `catalog_reference.sql`); shop page `/tienda/<slug>` with description and logo (`STATUS.md`); first name and district only (CLAUDE.md). Barcodes: label printing is a Launch feature, not built (`scope-by-app.md`); seller invoicing in the pilot (compliance §3). Whether an online-only seller can be in the Régimen Simplificado is open (compliance §10 q6), **for the accountant to confirm**; the shop does not yet show the notice compliance §3 requires for such a seller. "We help you list it" is true for any seller in the pilot (onboarding by the founder, roadmap phase 1); the free set-up of 20 products is a pricing proposal (README decision 2).

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Which company sentence goes on every piece?**
   - A. The long one above, with the short one for headers and bios. **(recommended)** The long one says what we do for both sides; the short one fits a 160-character bio.
   - B. The short one only.
2. **Which tagline in public?**
   - A. _Lo que ocupa, a su puerta._ only. **(recommended)** It is already in the product and the brand book; the three others still wait for a copy editor.
   - B. _Lo que ocupa, a su puerta._ for shoppers and _Usted vende. Nosotros lo llevamos._ for sellers, after the copy editor reads it.

The shop flyer's headline ("Usted vende. Nosotros guardamos, empacamos, entregamos y le cobramos al cliente.") is a sentence, not a tagline, but it is close to the second one; send it to the copy editor with the taglines.

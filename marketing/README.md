# Marketing and sales material

**What it is.** The words and the specifications for Yuntera's first marketing and sales pieces, in Spanish (usted) and English side by side. Written 24 November 2026. No piece is designed yet.
**Conclusion 1.** No piece may be printed, posted or sent before the trademark search is done and `yuntera.cr` is registered (gate G1). The brand book says so in section 15.
**Conclusion 2.** The seller pieces come first. Seller interviews may start in week 1 without printed material; visits, written yeses and print under the name wait for G1 and G2. Shopper pieces wait for real payments and a public shop.
**Conclusion 3.** Every claim here is checked against `STATUS.md` of 23 November 2026 and the code. A claim that depends on something not yet live names its gate. A claim the product cannot keep today is not made.
**Conclusion 4.** The first print run before the launch costs about ₡38,000–₡92,000, and the shopper flyer at the launch ₡30,000–₡60,000 more (assumptions; no price list was found; get 2 quotes).
**The owner must decide** nothing in this file directly. Every question these pieces depend on is asked once, in the [owner decisions](../docs/00-strategy/owner-decisions.md) list; the table at the end names the ones that change these pieces.

### How English is used

Every customer piece carries the English beside the Spanish so the owner, a partner or an English-reading customer can read it. **The Spanish is the version published in Costa Rica.** The shop's screens are Spanish only today, so an English piece meant for English readers must say "La tienda está en español por ahora. / The shop is in Spanish for now." until the English release. The English uses American spelling, pending owner decision D45.

### Money and dates

- Colones as the brand book writes them, `₡25 000`, in Spanish and in English copy, the same as the product's screens, receipts and invoices (owner decision D9; until it is answered, the copy follows its recommended option). Notes written for the owner keep `₡25,000`. The space in `₡25 000` is a narrow no-break space (U+202F) in the brand book, so a price never wraps; the source files type an ordinary space, and the designer sets U+202F in every amount before print.
- US dollars appear only in the investor brief, at **₡450 per US$**, the banks' selling rate of 18 November 2026 (market research §6.4), as in every strategy document. Re-check on the day of use.
- Dates as people say them: _lunes 7 de diciembre_.

## How results are measured

The product does not record where a customer came from (no "¿Cómo nos conoció?" field, no campaign tag; go-to-market §1). Until it does, each piece carries its own code, and the owner counts by hand in one spreadsheet every Saturday.

- **A WhatsApp link with a pre-written first line.** Each piece's QR code opens `wa.me/[número]?text=...` with a code in the text, for example "Hola, vengo del volante VENDE-1". The person sends it; the code arrives with the message. This needs nothing built.
- **A code to say out loud** at a fair or on the phone, printed beside the QR.
- **Codes are never personal.** They name the piece and the place, never a person.
- **The spreadsheet holds counts, not people.** Count returning shoppers by order number pairs, never by name, e-mail or phone.

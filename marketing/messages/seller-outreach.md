# Seller outreach and follow-up messages

**What it is.** The WhatsApp and e-mail texts a person at Yuntera sends to a shop: the first reply, the follow-ups, the yes, and the stop. With the consent rules that apply to marketing messages in Costa Rica.
**Conclusion 1.** Yuntera never writes first to a shop that has not asked. First contact is a visit, a call made by the owner, or a reply to a shop that wrote (a QR, a fair sheet, a referral the shop itself acted on).
**Conclusion 2.** Every message names the sender and offers a way to stop. A "no" or silence after two follow-ups ends the conversation.
**Conclusion 3.** These are sent by hand from the WhatsApp Business app and a normal mailbox; the product sends no marketing message yet (gate G10).
**The owner must decide** the question at the end (how many follow-ups).

**Gates:** G1, G2, G9 before any message. G12 for the consent rules (the lawyer confirms them).

---

## Consent rules

What the law says, from the primary text read by the market research (Ley 8642, Ley General de Telecomunicaciones, art. 44, SUTEL PDF, read 24 November 2026):

- Automated calls, fax, e-mail "o cualquier otro dispositivo" for direct sales are prohibited, except to subscribers who gave **prior consent**.
- Exception: a business that obtained a customer's e-mail with consent during a sale may use it to sell **similar** products, with a free way to stop at any time.
- Always prohibited: hiding the sender's identity, or giving no valid address to ask for the messages to stop.

What the product already requires (`docs/05-security-compliance/privacy.md`; `docs/06-operations/notifications.md`): commercial consent is separate, starts unticked, can be withdrawn at once, and every commercial message starts with "Publicidad" and carries a free opt-out. Commercial messages from the product are **not built**.

**For the lawyer to confirm:** whether a one-to-one WhatsApp or e-mail written by a person to a business counts under art. 44, and whether WhatsApp is "cualquier otro dispositivo". The safe reading, used here, is yes to both.

The rules this file follows:

| # | Rule | How it is checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Write only to a shop that asked: it wrote first, ticked the fair sheet's box, or said "escríbame" on a visit or call | The visit log has the date and the words |
| 2 | Never use bought, scraped or partner-supplied lists | No list exists to use |
| 3 | First line names the person and Yuntera; the footer gives the legal name | Every text below |
| 4 | Every message says how to stop; "no", "BAJA" or "no me escriba" ends it at once | The log marks "no contactar" with the date |
| 5 | At most two follow-ups after the first reply, at least 5 working days apart; then stop | The log |
| 6 | Write Monday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. (assumption: business hours; the notifications document's quiet hours apply to the product's own messages) | The sender |
| 7 | No buyer data, ID numbers or bank details in a message; documents go through the seller centre | The sender |
| 8 | No group broadcasts to shops; one person, one message | WhatsApp Business app, no broadcast lists for sellers |

Using the "Publicidad" prefix: a reply to a shop that asked for information is an answer, not an advert, so the texts below do not start with it. If the lawyer reads art. 44 as covering these replies, add "Publicidad:" at the start of messages 2 to 4 and of the e-mails.

## WhatsApp

### 1. Reply to a shop that wrote first (VENDE-1, VENDE-2, FERIA, RED codes)

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |
| Buenas, [nombre]. Soy [nombre], de Yuntera. Gracias por escribirnos. En resumen: usted nos manda el producto una vez y nosotros lo guardamos, lo empacamos, lo entregamos en [cantones] y cobramos por SINPE Móvil o contra entrega. Sin mensualidad: paga una comisión cuando vende. ¿Le mando la hoja con todas las tarifas y un ejemplo de lo que recibiría? | Hello, [name]. I'm [name], from Yuntera. Thank you for writing. Shall I send you the sheet with every fee and an example of what you would receive? |

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |

### 4. Second and last follow-up, 5 working days later

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |
| [Nombre], este es mi último mensaje. Si más adelante quiere vender con nosotros, escríbanos a este número. Gracias por su tiempo. | [Name], this is my last message. If you'd like to sell with us later, write to this number. Thank you for your time. |

### 5. After a yes: the steps

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |
| ¡Qué bueno! Estos son los pasos: 1. Regístrese en [vende.yuntera.cr] con la cédula de la tienda y una cuenta IBAN a su nombre. 2. Publique sus productos; antes de publicar verá cada cargo. 3. Reserve una hora para traer el producto a la bodega en [lugar]; cada unidad necesita su código de barras. Lo contamos y se lo confirmamos en un día hábil. Si se traba en algún paso, escríbame aquí. | Great! These are the steps: 1. Register at [vende.yuntera.cr] with the shop's ID and an IBAN account in its name. 2. We count it and confirm within one working day. If you get stuck at any step, write to me here. |

This message holds the piece's one exclamation (brand book §3: one, for good news).

### 6. When the shop says no or asks to stop

| Español (usted) | English |
| --- | --- |
| Entendido, [nombre]. No le vuelvo a escribir. Gracias. | Understood, [name]. I won't write again. Thank you. |

Then mark "no contactar" in the visit log with the date. Do not reply again unless the shop writes first.

## How the result is measured

| Measure | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Replies to message 1, by code | Visit log |
| Yes after message 2, 3 or 4 | Visit log |
| Registrations and shops active with stock | Console, **Vendedores** |
| Stops ("no", "BAJA") | Visit log, "no contactar" |
| Done when | Five shops active with stock (roadmap phase 0 exit) |

## Decision for the owner

1. **How many follow-ups after the fee sheet?**
   - A. Two on WhatsApp, at least 5 working days apart, one by e-mail; then stop. **(recommended)** It gives a busy shop a second chance without becoming the pressure the brand book forbids.
   - B. One follow-up only.
   - C. None: wait for the shop to write.
